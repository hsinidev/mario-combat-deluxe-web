Game Overview


The game Mario Combat Deluxe Web is a browser-based action-adventure game where you control Mario as he navigates through levels, fighting enemies and collecting power-ups to progress.



How to Play (Controls and Objective)


To play the game, simply click on the level tiles to enter them. Your goal is to collect all the coins scattered throughout the levels while avoiding obstacles like Goombas and Bullet Bills.


You can move Mario left or right using your mouse or arrow keys, and jump by pressing the space bar. You can also use power-ups like mushrooms and fire flowers to gain temporary advantages.



Key Controls:





Moving
: Left or Right arrow keys or Mouse wheel


Jumping
: Space bar


Fighting
: Click on enemies to attack


Power-ups
: Click on mushrooms and fire flowers


Coin collection
: Click on coins scattered throughout the levels


Level navigation
: Use level tiles or click on them to enter




Tips and Tricks for Success


Here are some tips to help you progress through the game:




Use power-ups strategically to gain an advantage over enemies.


Collect all coins to unlock special items or bonuses.


Learn enemy patterns to avoid taking damage from them.


Keep an eye on your health and use healing items when necessary.





Gameplay Strategies:





Boss battles: Use strategy and timing to defeat bosses like Bowser and Wario.



Rush hour: Rush through levels quickly to collect coins while avoiding obstacles.



Power-up chains: Collect power-ups in sequence to gain a significant advantage.
